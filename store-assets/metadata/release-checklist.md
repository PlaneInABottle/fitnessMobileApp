# Release Checklist

- [ ] Replace all TODO URLs and review contact fields.
- [ ] Confirm the final app name, bundle identifier, package name, version, and build numbers.
- [ ] Verify every exercise media asset is covered by the chosen license and include required attribution.
- [ ] Recheck Apple App Privacy and Google Data Safety answers against local workout and analytics behavior.
- [ ] Complete age/content ratings, export compliance, App Content, and review contact fields.
- [ ] Upload the 10 App Store screenshots in filename order.
- [ ] Upload up to 8 Google Play phone screenshots in filename order.
- [ ] Upload the Play icon and feature graphic.
- [ ] Upload the landscape preview to YouTube and add its public or unlisted URL to Google Play.
- [ ] Review the Apple preview poster frame at approximately 5 seconds.
- [ ] Build and test production iOS and Android binaries on physical devices.
- [ ] Verify offline launch, interrupted workouts, timer backgrounding, invalid numeric input, and local data persistence.
- [ ] Confirm deleting app data behaves as described in the privacy disclosures.
- [ ] Submit first to TestFlight and Google Play internal testing before production review.
