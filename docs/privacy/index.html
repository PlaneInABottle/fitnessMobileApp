Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

Fitness Tracker is an offline application. It does not require an account and does not send workout information to the developer or a remote service.

Data stored on your device

The app stores routines, workout sessions, exercise notes, set values, preferences, performance history, and personal records locally on your device so its features work.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Deletion and backups

You can delete the app to remove its locally stored data. Device-level backups are controlled by your operating system and account settings. Android app backup is disabled.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
