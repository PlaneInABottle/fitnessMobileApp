## Description

Fitness Tracker is a fast, private workout log that works without an account or internet
connection.

Start an empty workout or build reusable routines. Record weight, reps, timed sets, cardio
distance, warmups, working sets, and drop sets. Add exercise notes, review previous performance,
and use remembered values to prepare the next set.

After each session, see duration, completed sets, total volume, recent workouts, and personal
records on the Home dashboard. Your workout history remains on your device and is available even
when you are offline.

Key features:

- Offline workout tracking with no account required
- Built-in exercise library and custom routines
- Weight, reps, time, and distance tracking
- Warmup, working, and drop-set support
- Exercise notes and previous-performance suggestions
- Workout history, volume summaries, and personal records
- Light and dark appearance support

Fitness Tracker does not contain ads, analytics, or social tracking.

## Review Notes

The app is fully offline and does not require sign-in. Tap "Start workout," add an exercise, enter
set values, mark the set complete, and tap "Finish" to exercise the primary flow. Completed sessions
appear on the Home tab.
